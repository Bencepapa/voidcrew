## The product
"voidcrew" is a first-person grid dungeon-crawler engine (React + three.js) with two worlds: "Void Crew" (sci-fi ship decks) and "Void Fantasy" (dungeon, default). It has an in-game map editor that works inside the running 3D view (Tab toggles it): dig/fill walls, raise floors/ceilings, paint textures, and place props, decals, items, NPCs, lights, particle emitters, doors, ladders, bridges, exits, start, restricted cells, chance walls. Placed things have inspectable settings (chance, links, light colour/intensity/range, door lock, NPC wake-up alert level, smoke blow direction...).

## Direction from the owner (important)
1. The top bar does NOT have to be an opaque panel. Prefer a few familiar 3D-editor ICONS floating over the 3D view, arranged LEFT and RIGHT (tool icons on one side, view/file icons on the other), semi-transparent, appearing on hover/focus or kept very light. Think Blender / Unity / Godot / Unreal viewport overlays: small icon buttons with tooltips, active state highlighted.
2. The editor does NOT have to follow the game UI. Make it minimalist and compact: tiny paddings, small type, the 3D view gets the maximum screen. You may keep a subtle echo of our palette (dark brown #1a120a, gold #c9a24a, parchment yellow) for accents and the selection colour, but neutral dark glass panels are fine. Decide and justify.
3. Panels (asset tree, inspector) should be collapsible/auto-hiding and semi-transparent where they overlap the 3D view; Build mode keeps a big minimap on the left, 3D view on the right.
4. Consistent selection language: one outline colour per element category, the same on the 3D view, the 2D minimap, and the asset tree. Per-kind handles (see plan-v2.md section 8) - only 3D props get a (single ring) yaw rotation handle.
5. Desktop first (1440x860 and 1024x768), then tablet/phone landscape + portrait notes (bottom toolbar, inspector as bottom sheet, tree as drawer, no modifier keys: a Select button and long-press replace Alt).
